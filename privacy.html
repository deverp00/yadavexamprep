Privacy Policy

Learn how Yadav ExamPrep handles information when you access educational content and use its preparation tools.

1. Introduction

Yadav ExamPrep is a free online examination preparation platform designed to provide educational lessons, question practice and mock tests.

This policy describes the intended handling of information through the website, including browser storage, administrator access and supporting technical services.

2. Public Website Access

Visitors can browse published examinations, subjects, lessons and mock tests without creating an account or signing in.

Public access does not require visitors to submit their name, email address or phone number to use the educational features.

3. Browser Storage

Some website features use browser storage to maintain learning progress or display examination results.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Completed lesson identifiers and learning progress.
  • Mock-test answers and session results.
  • Other optional preparation preferences introduced by the website.

Browser storage is associated with the browser and device being used. Clearing website data may remove locally saved progress or results.

The current mock-test results feature uses session storage, while supported learning progress may use local storage.

4. Firebase Services

Yadav ExamPrep uses Google Firebase services to support website functionality.

Firebase Realtime Database provides published educational records to the public website. Firebase Authentication is used for protected administrator access.

These services may process technical information necessary to deliver requests, operate authentication and maintain service security.

For more information about Google's handling of data, consult the official Firebase Privacy Information .

5. Administrator Authentication

The administrator panel is restricted to authorized accounts. Authentication information is processed through Firebase Authentication.

Administrator permissions must also be enforced through Firebase Realtime Database Security Rules. Hiding administrator controls in the website interface alone does not secure database records.

6. Practice and Mock-Test Results

Practice answers are evaluated within the visitor's browser. The mock-test feature also calculates results locally and makes them available on the results page.

These tools are intended for self-assessment and learning. They are not designed as identity-verified or proctored examinations.

7. External Services and Links

The website may load resources from external providers, including font services, Firebase and the website's hosting provider.

When you follow an external link, the destination website may apply its own privacy practices and terms.

8. Information Security

The website is designed to separate public educational content from protected administrator records.

Appropriate authentication, database access rules and secure deployment practices are important parts of maintaining this separation.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Younger Learners

Educational content may be accessed by learners of different ages. Public educational features do not require account registration.

Parents and guardians may assist younger learners with browser privacy settings and the use of online services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised when website features, technical services or information-handling practices change.

The current version will be made available on this page.

11. Privacy Enquiries

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the website's information-handling practices, please visit the Contact page.
